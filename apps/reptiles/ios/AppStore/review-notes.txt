Rep-tiles is a self-contained puzzle game: cover a region exactly with tiles by drag and drop. It needs no account and has no sign-up, ads or in-app purchases. The game contacts no server of ours and works fully offline.

To try it: the first launch opens a four-step tutorial (drag, turn, flip, take off), each a tiny board; "Skip tutorial" leaves it, and How to play (the ? on the home screen) opens it again. After it, on iPhone the home screen offers Easy, Medium and Hard, which each deal a random level, and Tilings, which lists every tiling. On iPad a sidebar shows the same. Drag a tile from the tray at the bottom (or right) onto the board; it snaps into place where it fits. The round-arrow controls at the end of the tray turn every tray tile; drag a placed tile back over the tray to take it off. The bulb in the header shows a hint. A quick level to finish is Tilings › Regular polygons › 4⁴ (sixteen squares).

The privacy policy is in Settings (the gear on the home screen) › About › Privacy policy.

iCloud: the stars and best time earned on each level are kept in step between the user's devices through iCloud key-value storage, so they also survive a reinstall. With iCloud signed out or offline, everything works from the copy on the device.

The rating prompt is requested once, after the third finished board.

Haptic feedback is used on iPhone (Settings › Haptics turns it off).
