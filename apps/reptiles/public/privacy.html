Rep-tiles

Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

In short

  • Rep-tiles collects nothing. It sends no analytics, no play counts and nothing about you to any server.
  • Your stars, best times, boards in play and settings are kept in your browser, or on your computer in the desktop app, and never leave it.
  • No accounts, no ads, no cookies, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

On your device

The stars you have earned on each level, your best time on each, the tiles of a board you left half-covered, whether you have seen the tutorial and your choices in Settings are saved in your browser's local storage, and the game's files are cached so it works offline. None of that is sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes it.

Settings › Check for updates asks the website for its current version number. It sends nothing about you.

The desktop app

The desktop app is the same game, carried with everything it needs. It contacts no server at all, and keeps your stars, best times, boards in play and settings in its own folder on your computer.

Hosting

Cloudflare hosts the website at tiling-games.pages.dev. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details with each request, in order to deliver the pages and protect the site. It handles them under its own privacy policy. The game itself adds nothing to them.

Children

The game is for everyone. It asks for no personal information and collects none, from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Rep-tiles is open source, so every change to this page and to the code it describes is public.

Questions or requests: github.com/sirk0/hypersweeper/issues.
