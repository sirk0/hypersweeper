Hypersweeper is a self-contained puzzle game: minesweeper on many different surfaces and tilings. It needs no account and has no sign-up, ads or in-app purchases. The game contacts no server of ours and works fully offline.

To try it: on the home screen, tap Classic for the familiar square grid, Volumetric for a 3D cube of cubes, or 3D for a random surface or solid; Custom lists every board. Tap or click to reveal a cell; long-press or right-click to place a flag. Drag a 3D board to rotate it, and pinch or scroll to zoom. On Mac, the File menu picks the difficulty and the board.

Game Center: achievements use Game Center. If the device is not signed in, the app shows Apple's Game Center sign-in sheet at launch; the game is fully playable without signing in. To see an achievement unlock, win any board (Classic on Easy is quickest): "First clear" appears as a Game Center banner. Settings › Game Center opens the achievements page. The app only reports achievement progress, and reads nothing from Game Center except whether the player is signed in.

iCloud: best times and the achievement record are kept in step between the user's devices through iCloud key-value storage, so they also survive a reinstall. With iCloud signed out or offline, everything works from the copy on the device.

Stickers: the app includes an iMessage sticker pack of the boards, in Messages' app drawer.

The App Clip plays a single board (the chamfered dodecahedron, on Easy) and does not use Game Center or iCloud.

Haptic feedback is used on iPhone.
